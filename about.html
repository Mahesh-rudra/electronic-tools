About EE Calculator

Free electronics tools, explained simply

EE Calculator is a set of 20 free calculators for everyday electronics work: Ohm's law, voltage dividers, filters, 555 timers, op-amps, LED resistors, Zener and regulator heat, MOSFET power loss, thermal resistance, current sense resistors, battery packs, wire resistance, PCB trace width and component codes.

Each calculator draws a live circuit diagram as you type, and comes with a short "How it works" explanation, so you can see where the numbers come from instead of just copying an answer.

Who made it

EE Calculator is built by Mahesh under the Vedax Global name. I design and build electronics projects.

Who it's for

  • Engineering and diploma students checking lab and assignment work.
  • Hobbyists building Arduino and ESP32 projects.
  • Working professionals and circuit designers who need a quick, reliable check while designing.
  • Teachers who want a quick way to show how a circuit behaves.

A note on accuracy

The calculators use standard textbook formulas and assume ideal parts. Real components have tolerances, temperature effects and limits, so always check the datasheet before building anything critical.
